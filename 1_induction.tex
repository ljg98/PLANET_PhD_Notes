\begin{itemize}
    \item 40000 new people with nuclear skills needed, from apprentices to doctoral students.
    \item Funding for each PLANET project is split 40:40:20 between EPSRC DFA : Industry : University. The DFA funds the support roles, leaving the academic to the professors. 
    \item PLANET stipend is 10\% above the UK average.
    \item The ethos is not fixed, it will be co-created with the students via the setting up of a management board including two of the students. 
    \item Need to express barriers to full participation so that adjustments and ways forward can be considered. 
\end{itemize}
